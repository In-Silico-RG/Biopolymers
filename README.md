# Biopolymers — literature route for Camila Argel's undergraduate thesis

Broad and deep literature study on biopolymers, in three phases: bibliometric mapping of
the field, a sweep of computational chemistry, chemoinformatics and AI applied to
biomaterials and biopolymers, and a final narrowing to Latin America and Colombia.

Grupo de Investigación IN SILICO, Universidad de Sucre. Joint lines with GIFTEX, UNF and
Wilson Castro.

Start at `docs/00_plan.md`. Session history is in `docs/LOG.md`.
